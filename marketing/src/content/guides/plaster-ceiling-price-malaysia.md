A flat plaster ceiling in Malaysia costs roughly **RM5.50–12 per sq ft** in 2026, supplied and installed, according to
published contractor guides. L-boxes run at about **RM15–28 per ft run**, cove edges at **RM18–30 per ft run**, and
cornice from about **RM4 per ft run** for a plain profile. Painting, wiring and LED strips are usually extra, so a good
plaster ceiling quotation lists them as separate lines.

## Plaster ceiling price per sq ft in Malaysia (2026)

"Plaster ceiling" in Malaysia almost always means gypsum board screwed to a metal frame under the slab, with taped
joints and a skim coat. The flat ceiling is priced per square foot. Published rates vary by source, so here they are side
by side:

| Source (2026 unless stated) | Flat ceiling | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| plasterceiling.my | RM5.50 per sq ft | Contractor price list |
| Qanvast (Aug 2025) | RM6.50–7.50 per sq ft | Varies with materials and complexity |
| Hypro Renovation | RM7–9 per sq ft | Supply & install, KL area; 9mm board |
| ClickBina | RM8–12 per sq ft | Klang Valley, supply & install, excludes painting |

Moisture-resistant board, used in kitchens and bathrooms, costs a little more: Hypro puts it at **RM8.50–11 per sq ft**.
ClickBina suggests cement or calcium silicate board for wet areas.

What moves your rate up or down:

- **Ceiling height.** ClickBina says scaffolding for ceilings above 10 ft adds **10–20% to labour**.
- **Location and access.** Hypro places KL at the upper end, Selangor suburbs in the middle and smaller towns such as
  Ipoh and Seremban at the lower end. Lift limits and parking also add time.
- **Board grade and brand.** ClickBina puts branded boards at **15–30% more** than generic ones.
- **Design.** A two-tier or coffered ceiling runs at about **RM22–35 per sq ft**, and multi-tier feature ceilings at
  **RM35–50 per sq ft**, in ClickBina's figures.

## L-box, cove lighting and cornice: priced per foot run

Anything that follows the wall is measured in feet run (ft run), the length along the wall. Watch the unit when you
compare quotes, because some contractors price the same work per sq ft of design area.

| Cove (curved edge) | RM18–30 | per ft run | Hypro |
| Perimeter cove / L-box with light channel | RM15–25 | per sq ft of design area | ClickBina (LED separate) |

An L-box is the dropped box along the edge of the ceiling that hides wiring, pipes or a light strip. ClickBina gives a
typical drop of **100–250mm**. Cove lighting is the indirect light hidden in that channel.

## Extras to quote as separate lines

Most published flat-ceiling rates cover the frame, board, joint taping and skim coat, and nothing else. Hypro lists
wiring, downlight points, LED strips, the final paint and stripping an old ceiling as normally excluded. ClickBina
prices these extras as follows:

| Extra | Published rate | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Downlight or fan cut-out (ceiling labour only) | RM15–30 | per cut-out |
| Cove LED strip, supply & install | RM8–20 | per ft run of cove |
| LED driver / transformer | RM80–200 | per zone |
| Electrician's wiring | RM120–250 | per circuit |
| Hacking and disposal of old ceiling | RM2–5 | per sq ft |

Heavy fittings are another hidden cost. Qanvast notes that ceilings sometimes need reinforcing for heavy fixtures, so
ask about ceiling fans, chandeliers and cassette aircon units before you price.

## How to measure for a plaster ceiling quotation

1. **Flat ceiling, per sq ft.** Length × width of each room. A 20 × 15 ft living room is 300 sq ft. State whether
   you measure the full room or deduct the L-box area.
2. **L-box, cove and cornice, per ft run.** For a full perimeter, 2 × (length + width). The same 20 × 15 ft room
   has 70 ft run of perimeter.
3. **Cut-outs and lighting.** Count downlights, fan points and LED driver zones as numbered items, not as part of the
   sq ft rate.
4. **Wet areas separately.** Put kitchen and bathroom ceilings on their own lines with the moisture-resistant board
   named.
5. **Existing ceiling.** If an old ceiling has to come down first, add removal and disposal per sq ft.

## What to write in the specification

- **Board:** type and thickness (for example 9mm gypsum board, moisture-resistant board in wet areas), and the brand if
  the client cares.
- **Frame:** metal furring or a suspended grid. ClickBina recommends galvanised steel C-channel at 600mm centres over
  timber.
- **Finish:** joint taping and how many skim coats. ClickBina suggests two passes as a minimum.
- **Painting:** included or not, and how many coats.
- **Timeline:** ClickBina puts a 100–150 sq ft room at 1–2 days for framing, boarding and jointing, plus 1–2 days for
  skim and sanding after drying, and a whole apartment at 5–10 working days.
- **Exclusions and validity:** wiring, light fittings, scaffolding and how long the price holds.

If the client adds an L-box or more downlights after approving the quote, price the change in writing with a
[variation order](/guides/variation-order-template-malaysia) before the boards go up.
