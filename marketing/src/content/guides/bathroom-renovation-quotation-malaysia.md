A bathroom renovation in Malaysia costs roughly **RM3,000–8,000** for a cosmetic refresh, **RM8,000–18,000** for a
standard full renovation and **RM15,000–35,000** for a mid to high-spec bathroom in 2026. A good quotation breaks that
down into **hacking, waterproofing, tiling, plumbing and sanitary ware**, with the brands, sizes and quantities
spelled out. Below are the published rates, what to include, and a sample quotation.

## Bathroom renovation cost by scope (2026)

| Scope | Typical cost per bathroom | What it usually covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cosmetic refresh | RM3,000–8,000 | Re-grouting, new fittings, no hacking |
| Standard full renovation | RM8,000–18,000 | Hack, new waterproofing, retile, new sanitary ware |
| Mid to high-spec | RM15,000–35,000 | Feature or porcelain tiles, glass screen, rain shower |
| Premium | RM30,000–50,000+ | Large-format slabs, freestanding tub, new layout |

These ranges come from several 2026 guides, which group the tiers slightly differently. A full bathroom renovation
usually takes **10–15 working days**.

The biggest swings come from **moving plumbing points** and the **tile size**. Keeping the existing layout is the
cheapest way to do a full renovation.

## What to include in a bathroom quotation

- **Protection and permits**: floor and lift protection, and management approval in condos.
- **Hacking**: what's removed (floor tiles, wall tiles, existing fittings, bathtub) and debris disposal.
- **Screeding to falls**: so water drains to the floor trap.
- **Waterproofing**: the product, the coverage (floor, and walls up to what height) and the curing time before tiling.
  Quote a fresh membrane, not tiling over the old one.
- **Tiles**: size, type, and the area for floor and walls separately, with a wastage allowance.
- **Plumbing**: which points are kept and which are moved.
- **Sanitary ware and accessories**: brand and model, or "supplied by client, installation only".
- **Shower screen, ceiling, lighting and exhaust fan.**
- **Exclusions**: for example, water heater, mirror cabinet, or accessories not listed.

## Common bathroom quotation mistakes

- **"Waterproofing" with no detail.** Say what product, which surfaces and how high up the walls.
- **No curing time in the schedule**, which tempts the team to tile too early.
- **Unclear sanitary ware**: who supplies it, and which models.
- **One rate for floor and wall tiles** when the areas and tile types differ.
- **Forgetting screeding, disposal or the exhaust fan**, small lines that add up.
