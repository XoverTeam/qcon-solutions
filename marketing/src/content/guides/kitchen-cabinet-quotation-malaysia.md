A good kitchen cabinet quotation in Malaysia prices **base, wall and tall cabinets separately, per foot run**, and spells
out the **carcass board, door finish, hardware and countertop** on every line. In 2026, published rates run roughly
**RM180–300 per foot run for melamine**, **RM330–600 for plywood** and **RM400–700 for aluminium**. Below are the rates,
how to measure, what to specify, and a sample quotation you can follow.

## Kitchen cabinet price per foot run (2026)

| Cabinet material | Typical 2026 price per foot run | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Melamine on particle board | RM180–300 | Budget option, best for dry kitchens |
| Laminate (HPL) doors | RM280–420 | More scratch and heat resistant than melamine |
| Plywood carcass | RM330–600 | Holds screws and hinges better; common for wet kitchens |
| Aluminium | RM400–700 | Waterproof and termite-proof; premium ranges cost more |
| Tall cabinets (any material) | about RM450–690 | Priced higher per foot than base or wall units |
| Quartz countertop | from about RM150–180 | Priced per foot run of countertop |

Upgrading to premium hinges and drawer runners adds roughly **RM30–80 per foot run**. A typical custom set of 15–20 feet
of cabinets lands around **RM4,500–12,000** installed, depending on material, before countertop and appliances.

These are published market ranges, not a price list. Your own rates depend on your supplier, factory and location.

## How to measure a foot run

A **foot run** is one foot of cabinet length measured along the wall. It isn't an area, so the cabinet's height and
depth are built into the rate.

- **Measure base, wall and tall cabinets separately.** They have different rates, and a quotation that combines them
  can't be checked or compared.
- **Say how you count corners.** In an L-shaped kitchen, the corner can be counted in one run or both. Either is fine
  if it's written down. Unexplained corner charges are a common reason clients distrust cabinet quotes.
- **Leave out gaps.** The space for a freestanding fridge or a cooker hood without a cabinet isn't a foot run of cabinet.
- **Re-measure on site before fabrication.** Quote from the drawing, then confirm the final lengths on site, and price any
  difference at your quoted rate.

## What to specify on each line

Clients compare quotes line by line, and the cheapest quote often has the vaguest description. Spell out:

- **Carcass board**: melamine particle board, MDF, plywood or aluminium, and its thickness.
- **Door finish**: melamine, laminate (HPL), acrylic, spray paint or aluminium.
- **Hardware**: the hinge and drawer runner brand, and whether they're soft-close.
- **Countertop**: material, length and edge.
- **Cut-outs**: sink, hob and any socket openings.
- **Plinth, end panels and fillers**, if you charge for them separately.
- **Dismantling and disposal** of the old kitchen.
- **Installation and delivery**: included or charged separately.

## Common kitchen cabinet quotation mistakes

- **One lump sum for "kitchen cabinet".** The client can't compare it, and you can't price a change fairly.
- **No material spec.** "Cabinet" could mean melamine or plywood, a difference of hundreds of ringgit per foot.
- **Combining base and wall cabinets into one rate**, which hides how the price was worked out.
- **Forgetting dismantling, cut-outs or end panels**, small lines that add up to real margin.
- **No validity date**, so a client returns months later expecting the old board prices.
