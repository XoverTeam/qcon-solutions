An interior design quotation in Malaysia usually has two parts: the **design fee**, and the **design-and-build works**
grouped by **area or trade**, with a unit, quantity and rate on every line. Strong quotations also separate
**provisional sums** for items not yet chosen, list **loose furniture** on its own, and spell out **exclusions**, a
**payment schedule** and how **variations** are handled. Below is the format, the common fee models and a sample.

## How interior designers in Malaysia charge

| Fee model | How it works | Typical published range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Design fee per sq ft | Design and drawings only, priced on the built-up area | About RM3–8 per sq ft for homes |
| Percentage of project cost | Fee calculated on the renovation cost | Roughly 6–15%, with some guides quoting 10–20% |
| Design and build | Design fee folded into the build price, with a markup on works | Varies; often shown as "free design" |

Whichever model you use, say it plainly in the quotation. Clients comparing three IDs often can't tell whether the
cheapest quote includes design at all.

## Organise by area or by trade?

- **By area** (living, kitchen, master bedroom, bathrooms) is easier for homeowners to read and to cut when the budget
  is tight.
- **By trade** (hacking, tiling, carpentry, electrical, painting) is easier for you to cost and for contractors to
  price.

Many IDs do both: areas as the main sections, with each line naming the trade and specification. For example:
"Master bedroom: built-in wardrobe, 8 ft run, plywood carcass, laminate doors, soft-close hinges".

## What to include

1. **Design fee**: the model, and what's included (site measurement, layout, 3D renderings, construction drawings,
   number of revisions, site supervision).
2. **Build works by area or trade**, with unit, quantity, rate and a clear specification on every line.
3. **Provisional sums** for items still to be chosen: light fittings, wallpaper, sanitary ware, feature tiles.
4. **Loose furniture and decor**, listed separately if you supply them.
5. **Exclusions**: appliances, curtains, management deposits, authority fees, whatever isn't yours.
6. **Validity**: how long the prices hold.
7. **Payment schedule** linked to design and site milestones.
8. **Terms**: variations, defects period, ownership of drawings, and the estimated timeline.

## Sample interior design quotation (summary page)

A 1,000 sq ft condo, design and build. The figures are **illustrative**.

| Section | Amount (RM) |
| --- | --- |
| A. Design fee: layout, 3D, construction drawings, 2 revisions (1,000 sq ft @ RM5) | 5,000.00 |
| B. Preliminaries: protection, management permit, site cleaning | 1,800.00 |
| C. Living & dining: feature wall, TV console, plaster ceiling, lighting points | 18,500.00 |
| D. Kitchen: cabinets, quartz countertop, wall tiles, electrical points | 16,200.00 |
| E. Master bedroom: wardrobe, bed headboard panel, ceiling, lighting | 14,300.00 |
| F. Bedrooms 2 & 3: wardrobes, study table, lighting | 15,600.00 |
| G. Bathrooms ×2: hacking, waterproofing, tiles, screens | 26,000.00 |
| H. Painting, whole unit | 6,500.00 |
| I. Provisional sums: light fittings RM3,000, wallpaper RM1,200 | 4,200.00 |
| **Total** | **108,100.00** |

Each section then has its own detailed page of line items. The summary page is what the client reads first, and it's
where you decide what they'll compare you on.

Loose furniture, if you supply it, works best as a separate quotation, so the client can approve the renovation
without waiting on every sofa choice.

## A payment schedule that fits design work

| Stage | Milestone | % |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Design fee on appointment | Design fee |
| 2 | Deposit on confirmation of the build quotation | 10–20% of build |
| 3 | Wet works and electrical completed | 30% |
| 4 | Carpentry installed | 30% |
| 5 | Handover | Balance, less retention |
| 6 | Retention after the defects period | About 5% |

Homeowner guides treat a 10–20% build deposit as normal and flag anything above 25% upfront as a warning sign. Taking
the design fee upfront protects your design time if the client doesn't go ahead. The [renovation payment schedule guide](/guides/renovation-payment-schedule-malaysia) covers
payment terms and retention in more detail.

## Common interior design quotation mistakes

- **"Free design" with no scope**, so the client doesn't know what they'll get, or that the fee is in the build price.
- **Lump sums per room** ("Master bedroom – RM14,300") with no breakdown.
- **No provisional sums**, so lighting and finishes become surprise costs later.
- **Vague carpentry**: no board, finish or hardware spec.
- **No clause on drawings**, so the client can take your design to a cheaper contractor.
