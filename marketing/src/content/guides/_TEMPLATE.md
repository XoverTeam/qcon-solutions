Start with a two- or three-sentence answer to the question in the title. AI tools and Google
quote this first paragraph most often, so make it complete on its own.

## A clear section heading

Write in plain language. Use real numbers and Malaysian examples (RM, local trades, typical rates).

- Bullet lists work
- **Bold** for key terms
