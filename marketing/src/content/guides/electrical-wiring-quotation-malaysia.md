Rewiring a home in Malaysia costs roughly **RM3,000–12,000 for a 2–3 bedroom condo**, **RM5,000–15,000 for a
single-storey terrace** and **RM8,000–20,000 for a double-storey terrace** in 2026, according to published price
guides. Individual points run at about **RM95–150 for a 13A socket** and **RM95–140 for a lighting point**, and a new
single-phase DB box adds about **RM1,500–3,500**. A good electrical wiring quotation prices each of these as a separate
line, so the client sees what they are paying for.

## Rewiring cost in Malaysia by home type (2026)

A full rewire means new cables, sockets, switches and lighting points throughout, usually with a new distribution
board (DB box). Published ranges differ by source, so here they are side by side:

| Home type | ClickBina (2026) | wiringrumah.my (Jul 2026) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Studio or small apartment (under 700 sq ft) | RM4,000–8,000 | – |
| Apartment or 2–3 bedroom condo | RM6,000–12,000 | RM3,000–6,000 |
| Single-storey terrace | RM8,000–15,000 | RM5,000–10,000 |
| Double-storey terrace | RM12,000–20,000 | RM8,000–15,000 |
| Semi-D or bungalow | RM15,000–30,000+ | RM12,000–25,000+ |

A partial rewire, such as one section, the kitchen or added points in the living areas, costs about **RM1,000–5,000**
in both wiringrumah.my's and iHome.my's 2026 figures. iHome.my puts a full rewire at **RM4,000–15,000** for a whole
house, and the itemised costs PropertyGuru cites from iDFAC Engineering put it at **RM3,500–10,000+** per project.

What moves the price:

- **Number of points.** reno.my estimates about **35–50 points** for a three-bedroom flat and **60–90 points** for a
  full rewire of a double-storey terrace.
- **Concealed or surface wiring.** ClickBina prices a socket on surface trunking near the DB at **RM120–250**, but a
  concealed (chased-in) socket at **RM200–400**, because the wall has to be cut and patched.
- **Cable run length.** A socket on a long run far from the board costs **RM350–600** in ClickBina's figures.
- **DB box.** Replacing the board is often the single biggest line (see below).

water heater**. If you quote this way, say clearly in the quotation that cable, sockets and fittings are excluded.

## DB box, RCCB and MCB costs

The DB box (distribution board, also called the consumer unit) holds the main switch, the RCCB that trips on earth
leakage, and the MCBs that protect each circuit. ClickBina's 2026 Klang Valley ranges:

| Item | Published rate |
| --- | --- |
| Add or replace a single RCCB, MCB or RCBO | RM250–600 |
| Standard single-phase consumer unit upgrade | RM1,500–3,500 |
| Split-load board with two RCCBs | RM2,000–4,000 |
| Three-phase board | RM3,500–8,000+ |
| Fault-finding or tripping diagnosis | RM150–400 |
| Electrical inspection with test report, condo or small unit | RM150–400 |
| Electrical inspection with test report, landed house | RM400–900 |

reno.my lists a new distribution board with MCBs and an RCD as part of a full rewire, along with insulation testing and
a completion certificate. If your price includes the board, name it on its own line. If it doesn't, write that the
existing board is retained.

## Who can do the wiring

Electrical work is regulated by the Energy Commission (Suruhanjaya Tenaga), not by CIDB alone. The Commission says
contractors and wiremen must hold a valid licence it has issued before carrying out electrical installation work.
Under the Electricity Regulations 1994, as published by the Commission:

- **Regulation 75**: electrical work may only be carried out by a holder of a valid Certificate of Registration as an
  Electrical Contractor.
- **Regulation 12**: wiring on a single-phase supply must be under the immediate supervision of a qualified wireman,
  employed full-time by the electrical contractor, who certifies a Supervision and Completion Certificate when the
  work is finished.
- **Regulations 13 and 14**: the installation must then be tested by an authorised wireman, who certifies a Test
  Certificate. These are Forms G and H.

For a renovation contractor, this usually means subcontracting the electrical package to a registered electrical
contractor, or holding that registration yourself, and handing the client the certificates at completion. You can
check a contractor on the Energy Commission's list of registered electrical contractors. CIDB also has a separate
specialisation code for wiring (E04); see the [CIDB registration guide](/guides/cidb-registration-malaysia).

_This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current rules with the Energy Commission._

This is an **illustrative** quotation for a full rewire of a three-bedroom condo with 39 points, inside reno.my's
35–50 range. Every rate sits inside the published ranges above. Use your own rates.

| Lighting point with switch, cable included (fitting excluded) | point | 16 | 110.00 | 1,760.00 |
| 13A socket point, cable and back box included | point | 18 | 120.00 | 2,160.00 |
| Aircon point with dedicated RCD | point | 3 | 230.00 | 690.00 |
| Water heater point with isolator | point | 2 | 250.00 | 500.00 |
| Single-phase consumer unit (DB box) upgrade, with RCCB and MCBs | lot | 1 | 2,200.00 | 2,200.00 |
| Inspection and test report | lot | 1 | 300.00 | 300.00 |
| **Total** | | | | **7,610.00** |

**Excluded:** light fittings, ceiling fans, aircon units and water heaters; wall patching and repainting after
chasing; TNB applications for a supply upgrade; and management deposits. The total works out to about **RM195 per
point** across the 39 points, and sits inside ClickBina's RM6,000–12,000 range for a 2–3 bedroom condo.

## What to put in an electrical wiring quotation

- **A point schedule by room.** reno.my's per-room estimates are a useful check: about 8–12 points in the living
  room, 10–14 in the kitchen, 6–9 per bedroom, 2–4 per bathroom and 4–8 for the exterior and staircase.
- **The type of each point.** Separate 13A sockets, lighting points, and aircon and water heater points, because the
  rates differ.
- **Cable and wiring method.** reno.my suggests stating the cable type. Say whether the wiring is concealed or in
  surface trunking.
- **The DB box.** New or retained, single- or three-phase, and how many RCCBs.
- **Testing and certification.** Name the test report and the certificates you will hand over.
- **Making good.** Who patches and paints the chased walls. wiringrumah.my lists drilling and wall finishing among the
  items a full quotation should cover.

If the client adds points after the walls are closed, price them with a
[variation order](/guides/variation-order-template-malaysia) before the work starts. Commercial jobs may also attract
service tax if you are registered; see the [SST guide](/guides/sst-renovation-construction-malaysia).
